Fuzzy-logic race car

A self-driving race car built around a fuzzy logic controller in C#. Speed and track curvature feed a set of fuzzy rules that output throttle and steering values, letting the car brake into turns, hold a line through them, and accelerate back out — without any hard-coded if/else driving logic.
